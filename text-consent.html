Text Messaging with AS CPA

AS CPA offers text messaging to clients for account-related communications, including appointment scheduling, document requests, tax payment reminders, filing updates, and conversations with our staff.

How to sign up

Clients may request text messaging by calling our office at (845) 425-2024, speaking with a staff member in person, or signing a separate SMS consent authorization.

To use the written authorization, print it, sign and date it, and send it to us by email (a scan or clear photo) to office@ascpapc.com, or by mail to Aaron Sendrovits CPA PLLC, 1 Hillcrest Center Dr, Ste 312, Spring Valley, NY 10977.

After you provide consent, you will receive a confirmation text. No reply is required to complete your enrollment.

Message frequency and charges

Message frequency varies based on your account activity and communications with our office. Message and data rates may apply.

Opting out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us at (845) 425-2024.

Consent to receive text messages is optional and is not a condition of receiving our accounting or tax services.

We do not sell or share your mobile number or SMS consent information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

See our Privacy Policy and Text Messaging Terms for additional information.
