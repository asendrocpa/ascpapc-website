Information we collect

We collect information you provide to us to deliver accounting, tax and advisory services, such as your name, address, email address, phone and mobile numbers, tax and financial records, and information about your spouse, dependents or business when it is needed for our work.

How we use it

We use your information only to provide and manage our services to you: preparing returns and financial statements, communicating with you about your account, sending reminders you request, scheduling, billing, and meeting our legal and professional obligations.

How we protect and share it

Client information is confidential. We do not sell or rent personal information. Your choices

Text Messaging Consent and Terms

AS CPA (Aaron Sendrovits CPA PLLC) sends nonmarketing text messages to clients who have requested or authorized this communication method.

Consent methods

Clients may consent verbally during a telephone conversation or in person, or by signing a separate SMS consent authorization.

For verbal enrollment, an AS CPA representative confirms the client's permission and records the mobile number, date, time, consent method, and staff member responsible for enrollment. Telephone calls do not need to be recorded.

For signed enrollment, the client returns the signed authorization by email (a scan or clear photo) to office@ascpapc.com, or by mail to Aaron Sendrovits CPA PLLC, 1 Hillcrest Center Dr, Ste 312, Spring Valley, NY 10977. AS CPA retains a copy of the signed authorization.

After enrollment, AS CPA sends an automatic confirmation text identifying the messaging program and explaining how to opt out or obtain assistance. No reply to this confirmation is required.

Types of messages

Messages may include appointment scheduling, tax reminders, requests for documents, filing updates, and other communications relating to the client's account. We do not send marketing texts.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is optional and is not a condition of receiving services.

Opt-out and assistance

Clients can reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe or HELP for assistance. Clients may also contact our office at (845) 425-2024 or office@ascpapc.com. After replying STOP you will receive one confirmation and no further texts; reply START to resubscribe.

We do not sell or share mobile phone numbers or SMS consent information with third parties or affiliates for promotional or marketing purposes. Information may be processed by service providers solely to deliver and support our messaging services.

SMS communications may not be appropriate for transmitting sensitive tax or financial information. We may direct clients to a secure document exchange method when appropriate.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
